Concept notes

This page redesigns the homepage around three goals: help people who don't have a destination yet, make seclusion and nature the main search criteria, and turn the nature-project promise into something guests can see. The numbers match the pins on the page.

1

Search-first hero

Three fields, with "Anywhere in nature" and flexible dates as defaults.

Measure: share of visits that search
2

Nature-type chips

Start from forest, water or dark skies instead of a town name.

Measure: searches using a nature filter
3

Trust strip

Four reassurances directly under the search, including a clear total price.

Measure: bounce rate on the homepage
4

Browse by landscape

Image tiles for people still choosing what kind of trip they want.

Measure: clicks from tiles to results
5

Available-now listings

Homes free this weekend near the visitor, with quick date tabs.

Measure: last-minute bookings
6

"Nearest neighbour" on cards

Make seclusion something you can see and compare, and show the total stay price.

Measure: click-through from cards
7

Map as a main entry point

Nature layers and draw-to-search, because location is the product.

Measure: map sessions that lead to a booking
8

Visible impact

A named project near the stay, with progress, instead of a line in the footer.

Measure: booking rate, repeat guests
9

Seasonal ideas linked to homes

Every inspiration block ends in bookable homes.

Measure: content-to-booking path
10

Clear host path

One host section and a header link, kept apart from the guest flow.

Measure: host sign-ups
Check against the live site before sharing: this concept was made without access to nature.house, so compare it with the current homepage and adjust. Confirm the current home count, the exact nature-project contribution model and partner names (sources from 2020–2023 disagree), brand colours and logo, and whether listings already show a seclusion or distance measure.
