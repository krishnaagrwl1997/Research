Meri Toh Alag Hai · मेरी तो अलग है

A 2D illustrated video that plays like a desi pop-art zine coming to life. One original heroine, the "maharani of her own chaos". Every lyric line is its own poster scene on a flat, loud background, and the bold lyrics are part of the artwork. Each time the chorus says "alag", the art style switches between the four reference looks.

Production notes

  1. Add lyrics in the edit (CapCut, After Effects or Premiere), never in AI-generated frames. AI image tools still get Devanagari wrong.
  2. Keep the heroine consistent. Make a character sheet first (front, side and three-quarter views, plus hands), then use it as the reference image for every shot.
  3. The outro is very short. The track ends at about 1:31 and the spoken outro gets roughly 3 seconds. Consider adding a 3–4 second tail of room tone or a beat loop so the "cancel" stamp and the end card have room to breathe.
  4. Food-delivery app (shot 21): draw a generic orange app with no real logo, so the video doesn't use another brand's marks.
  5. Shorts and Reels: keep the chorus shots (13–17) centred so they crop cleanly to 9:16.
  6. Thumbnail: use shot 20's palm-to-camera pose on hot pink, with a huge halftone "अलग".
